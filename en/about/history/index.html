History of this domain

How goodgoodmart.com was used before, and how this site relates to it.

Why this page exists

A completely different service used to occupy this domain. People still arrive here from articles and links written at that time, so the history should be stated plainly.

The previous occupant

From 2021 to 2023, goodgoodmart.com was used by an online store called Good Good Mart. Built around “social good” and ethical themes, it sold plant-based food, allergen-free groceries, vegan cosmetics and environmentally considered household goods.

It was operated by Frembassy Inc., which had run a plant-based information portal called Vegewel since 2016. Good Good Mart launched in January 2021 as a renaming of the company's earlier “Vegewel Marché”. In February 2022 Frembassy joined the euglena Co., Ltd. group.

Good Good Mart closed in 2023, and the domain registration subsequently lapsed.

How this site relates to it

Ethical Pantry is an entirely separate publication, run by Deepak Banga, who acquired the domain after it lapsed.

  • We have no ownership, personnel or commercial relationship with Frembassy Inc. or euglena Co., Ltd.
  • We are not a successor to the Good Good Mart business
  • We do not use the former site's brand name, logo, product information or content
  • We do not sell anything

If you followed a link to the old store

Links to the former store's product and category pages are redirected to the closest relevant page here. The products themselves are not available on this site.

We cannot help with orders, returns or deliveries from the former store.

Why use this domain at all

A large number of articles about plant-based and ethical topics still link here. Running an information site in the same field means those links reach something relevant rather than a dead page.

At the same time, to avoid any confusion with the former brand, this site is named Ethical Pantry and every page links to this explanation.
