Beauty

An entry point to skincare, haircare, body care and makeup.

How to think about this area

Beauty has better disclosure rules than food, but words like “organic” and “natural” still carry no legal definition here. It helps to separate what the system guarantees — full ingredient lists — from what you have to verify yourself: certification, and where ingredients come from.

Into the categories

  • Skincare: lotions, serums, creams
  • Haircare: shampoo and conditioner
  • Body care: soap, oils, lip balm
  • Makeup: pigments and textures
  • Cosmetics: reading a full ingredient list
