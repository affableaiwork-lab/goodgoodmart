Plant milks

Soy, oat, almond and other plant-based drinks. Each suits different uses.

Character by type

  • Soy: highest in protein. Unadjusted and adjusted versions differ in taste and use
  • Oat: handles heat well and resists splitting in coffee. Higher in carbohydrate
  • Almond: low calorie, low protein
  • Rice: mild and naturally sweet, few allergens
  • Coconut: high fat, better for cooking than drinking

What to check

  • Sweetened or unsweetened
  • Whether calcium or vitamins are added
  • Emulsifiers and stabilisers
  • Whether it is a barista formulation
