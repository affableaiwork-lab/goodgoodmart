Everyday essentials

Detergents, cleaning products, paper goods. High volume means small choices add up.

The compounding category

These are bought repeatedly and used in quantity, so a small per-unit difference becomes a large total. Refills, concentrates and solid formats all cut packaging directly.

For paper goods, the questions are feedstock — recycled content, FSC-certified fibre — and the bleaching method.

What to check

  • Whether refills or concentrates are sold
  • Recycled content and FSC certification on paper goods
  • Biodegradability of detergents
  • Fragrance strength, and whether an unscented option exists
