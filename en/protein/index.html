Protein

Plant protein powders and protein-rich foods. Source changes both taste and nutrition.

Differences by source

  • Soy: good amino acid balance, reasonably priced
  • Pea: low allergenicity, distinctive flavour
  • Rice: gentle on digestion, lower in lysine on its own
  • Hemp: brings fibre and minerals, lower protein purity
  • Blends: designed so the sources complement each other

What to check

  • Protein per serving, and as a share of total weight
  • Which sweetener
  • Added vitamins and minerals
  • How it combines with whole-food protein: beans, nuts, whole grains
