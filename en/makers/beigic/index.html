BEIGIC

The brand

BEIGIC is a Korean skincare brand whose formulations centre on compounds derived from unroasted green coffee beans. It presents itself as vegan and entered the Japanese market in 2020.

Its best-known products are face oils such as the Lucent Oil, and it appears regularly in Japanese beauty coverage and vegan cosmetics round-ups.

Choosing

Even with a brand that states a vegan formulation, reading the full ingredient list once is worth doing. Cosmetics sold in Japan are required to disclose every ingredient.

Product range and Japanese availability change. Check the official site for current information.
