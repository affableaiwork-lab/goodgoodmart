COCOWELL

An Osaka-based coconut specialist, making everything from food to body care from coconut.

The company

COCOWELL Inc. is a coconut specialist headquartered in Osaka. Its range runs from foods — coconut oil, coconut milk, coconut sugar — through to body care products such as lip balm.

Coconut is an unusually complete raw material: flesh, shell and fibre all have uses, so little is left over. Companies working with it exclusively tend to build their ranges around that.

Coverage

Its coconut-oil lip balm has been featured in several Japanese outlets, including business media.

The product range changes. Check the official site for current information.
