Makers

VEGHEET

Thin sheets made from out-of-spec vegetables, produced by Takeshita Sangyo in Hirado City, Nagasaki.

At a glance
ProducerTakeshita Sangyo Co., Ltd.
LocationHirado City, Nagasaki
FieldVegetable sheets / food loss reduction

What it is

VEGHEET is made by pureeing vegetables, drying them and forming them into thin sheets. Carrot, tomato and spinach each give a different colour and flavour, and the sheets are used as wrappers or as a garnish.

The raw material is vegetables rejected on size or shape. Giving a use to produce that is perfectly good but hard to sell has made it a frequently cited example of food loss reduction in Japanese media.

Background

It is manufactured by Takeshita Sangyo Co., Ltd. of Hirado City, Nagasaki. The company's original expertise is in seaweed processing, and VEGHEET began as an application of that drying and forming technology to vegetables.

Product range and specifications change. Check the official site for current information.
