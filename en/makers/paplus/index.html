Makers

PAPLUS

A plastic alternative made from paper and plant-derived material, developed by Camino Inc.

At a glance
CompanyCamino Inc.
FieldPlastic alternative materials

The material

PAPLUS combines paper with plant-derived material and is notable for its high bio-based content. It was developed as a replacement for single-use plastic items.

It is used in cutlery and packaging, and adopted by businesses working to reduce plastic.

Choosing

Being plant-derived and being biodegradable are different properties. With materials of this kind, check the conditions required for breakdown — home compost or industrial facility — and whether your municipality has a waste category for it.

Material specification and product range change. Check the official site for current information.
