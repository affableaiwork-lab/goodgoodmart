Fermenstation

A company upcycling unused resources through fermentation, starting with ethanol made from organic rice grown on fallow paddies in Oshu, Iwate.

At a glance
CompanyFermenstation Co., Ltd.
SourcingOshu City, Iwate
FieldFermentation / upcycled materials

What the company does

Fermenstation Co., Ltd. uses its own fermentation technology to turn previously unused resources into materials. At the centre of this is a rice ethanol fermented from organic rice grown on fallow paddy fields in Oshu City, Iwate.

The rice lees left by fermentation are not discarded either — they go into cosmetic ingredients and animal feed. Using both the input and its by-products is what distinguishes the company's approach to upcycling.

Where you meet it

Its materials are supplied to cosmetics and household goods manufacturers, and it also sells under its own brand. It is one of the more widely stocked names in Japanese ethical retail.
