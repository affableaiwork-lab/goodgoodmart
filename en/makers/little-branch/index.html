Little Branch

An Ehime-based brand working with local forest resources — log-grown shiitake and cedar products.

At a glance
BasedEhime Prefecture
FieldLog-grown shiitake / woodwork

The brand

Little Branch works in Ehime Prefecture, making products from the region's forest resources. Its range includes log-grown shiitake mushrooms and plates made from cedar.

Log cultivation takes considerably more time and labour than growing shiitake on sawdust blocks. Because it uses timber that comes out of forest maintenance, it sits between forestry and food.

Background

The brand's stated theme is connecting Ehime's forests to daily life, which is why it carries both food and woodwork.

The product range changes. Check the official site for current information.
