Food

An entry point to the food categories.

Where the information is

In food, the ingredient list and the allergen line carry the most information. Marketing terms — plant-based, additive-free, superfood — mostly lack legal definitions, while Organic JAS and allergen labelling are backed by regulation. Keeping that distinction in mind makes decisions faster.

Main categories

  • Plant-based, soy meat, plant milks
  • Allergen-free and gluten-free
  • Organic, additive-free, no added MSG
  • Seasonings, staples, sweets, drinks
  • Food loss reduction and welfare workplace partnerships
